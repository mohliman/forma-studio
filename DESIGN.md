# FORMA — architectural monograph

The audience is prospective employers, agencies and design-minded business owners. The work should demonstrate composition and understandable implementation, rather than make unsupported claims about a fictional practice.

## Identity

Treat the site as an architectural publication: a large FORMA masthead, an unbroken concept image and small, readable marginal annotations. Warm paper, ink and a single oxidised red index colour establish the palette. DM Sans is the precise supporting voice; Bodoni Moda gives titles an architectural book character. All fonts remain locally bundled with their existing licences.

Use a twelve-column desktop grid. Reserve a narrow rail for figure numbers and captions; vary image spans to suit the subject. Section spacing follows an eight-pixel rhythm, with 48/80/112-pixel intervals. Body text is 14–16 pixels; labels are at least 11 pixels. Mobile cover layouts put the masthead, short positioning line, image and project caption in a deliberate reading order. Exterior views keep a wider crop; interior views can occupy a taller frame.

## Three redesigns

1. Replace the slogan-led homepage with a monograph cover. The name becomes the dominant typographic element, while Courtyard House provides the actual visual argument. Selected concepts become different-sized editorial plates.
2. Replace identical project pages with three compositions: courtyard, horizon and room. Each has an explicit project-specific focal crop, title arrangement, material colours and short figure annotation. Every project still exposes the same information and next-project navigation.
3. Replace the repeated giant enquiry slogan with a publication colophon: a small invitation, a clear demo link and an honest portfolio note. Rewrite the studio process as a numbered reading sequence rather than four feature cards.

## Interaction and motion

The signature interaction is an accessible full-view/detail control. It focuses attention on a project's chosen material or spatial detail without implying a second photograph. The crop changes with a restrained transform. Keyboard focus and selected state explain the control; reduced motion makes the change immediate. No scroll hijacking, compulsory loading or custom cursor. Remove blanket section and route fade/slide animations: the image control, link rules and navigation opening provide sufficient movement. All essential text is always visible.
